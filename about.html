About MetroGym

How We Started

From One Room To A Community

MetroGym opened its doors on Cape Road, Gqeberha in 2016, founded by Asavela Kahla with a simple idea: fitness works best when it's coached, measured, and shared with other people. What started as a single training room has grown into a full functional fitness facility serving the local community.

We focus on functional, CrossFit-style training — movements that build strength you actually use outside the gym. Every session is coach-led, scalable to any fitness level, and built around a class culture where members push each other.
